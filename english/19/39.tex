\begin{question}{79}
다음 글을 읽고 밑줄 친 문장과 의미가 가장 가까운 것을 고르시오.
\begin{framedtext}
Modern history begins when history becomes concerned with the future as well as the past. Modern man peers eagerly back into the twilight out of which he has come, in the hope that its faint beams will illuminate the obscurity into which he is going; conversely, his aspirations and anxieties about the path which lies before him sharpen his insight into what lies behind. \uline{No consciousness of the future, no history.} Between past and future, action and interaction are constant. Past, present, and future are woven together in an endless chain.
\end{framedtext}
\begin{choices}
  \item History is unconscious of the future.
  \item History is disconnected from the future.
  \item Future historians must have a conscience.
  \item The future requires historical consciousness.
  \item History presupposes the consciousness of the future.
\end{choices}
\end{question}
\answer{5}
